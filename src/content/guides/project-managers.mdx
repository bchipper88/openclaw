## How AI is Changing Project Management

Project management has always been about juggling information — status updates, risk registers, stakeholder communications, resource plans. AI doesn't replace the strategic thinking, but it dramatically reduces the time spent on information synthesis and reporting.

### The Numbers Behind AI-Powered PM

The Project Management Institute's latest research reveals that **65% of project managers** now use AI tools regularly. Teams that integrate AI into their PM workflows see:

- **8+ hours saved per week** on status reporting and documentation
- **40% faster** identification of project risks and blockers
- **3x more consistent** stakeholder communication cadence

### Where AI Makes the Biggest Impact

The highest-ROI use cases for project managers aren't the flashy ones. They're the repetitive tasks that eat your calendar:

1. **Status report generation** — Pull data from Jira or Asana and transform it into exec-ready updates
2. **Meeting intelligence** — Convert transcripts into action items, owners, and deadlines automatically
3. **Risk assessment** — Feed project context to Claude and get structured risk analysis in minutes
4. **Stakeholder emails** — Draft communications tailored to different audiences from the same project data

Our guide walks through each of these workflows with exact prompts, tool configurations, and Zapier automations you can set up in an afternoon.
