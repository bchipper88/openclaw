## Your Open-Source Marketing Engine

Marketing teams produce enormous volumes of content, manage multiple channels, and constantly analyze performance data. OpenClaw is a free, open-source AI agent (165,000+ GitHub stars, MIT license) that runs locally on your machine and automates the repetitive parts of marketing work. It connects to WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, a web UI, and an API, and supports Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and local models like LLaMA and Mistral.

With OpenClaw running 24/7 on your own hardware, you get an always-available marketing assistant that remembers your brand voice, campaign history, and content strategy across every conversation. No monthly AI subscription fees, and your proprietary marketing data never touches a third-party cloud.

### Content Repurposing Skill

Install the **Content Repurposer** skill from ClawHub, OpenClaw's marketplace of over 5,700 community skills. Feed it a long-form blog post, webinar transcript, or whitepaper, and it generates a LinkedIn post, a Twitter thread, an email newsletter snippet, Instagram caption, and a short-form video script -- all matched to your brand voice. One piece of content becomes ten in minutes instead of hours.

Because OpenClaw has persistent memory, it learns your tone, preferred hashtags, audience segments, and formatting preferences over time. The more you use it, the less you need to edit its output.

### Social Media Scheduler Agent

Connect OpenClaw to your social platforms through their APIs and set up an autonomous scheduling agent. It can draft a week's worth of social posts based on your content calendar, queue them for optimal posting times, and adjust copy for each platform's character limits and audience expectations. Review the batch through Slack or the web UI, approve with a single message, and the posts go out on schedule.

Combine this with the content repurposing skill to create a pipeline: publish a blog post, and OpenClaw automatically generates and schedules the social promotion across all your channels.

### SEO Analysis

The **SEO Analyst** skill on ClawHub reads your existing content and provides keyword gap analysis, meta description suggestions, internal linking recommendations, and content structure improvements. Point it at a URL or paste in draft content, and it returns actionable optimization recommendations grounded in current search best practices.

Run a weekly SEO audit by scheduling OpenClaw to crawl your published pages and flag opportunities for improvement. The results arrive in your Slack channel every Monday morning.

### Campaign Performance Reporting

Connect OpenClaw to your analytics platforms via API and automate campaign reporting. It pulls data from Google Analytics, ad platforms, email tools, and social dashboards, then compiles everything into a narrative report that explains what happened, why it matters, and what to do next. Schedule reports for weekly, monthly, or end-of-campaign cadences.

OpenClaw's persistent memory means it compares current performance against historical baselines without you providing the context each time. Trend analysis and anomaly detection happen automatically.

### Getting Started

Install OpenClaw from GitHub, connect Slack for team collaboration, set up API keys for your marketing stack, and browse ClawHub for marketing skills. Full setup in an afternoon, zero cost, and complete control over your data.
