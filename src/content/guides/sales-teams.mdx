## An Always-On Sales Intelligence Agent

Sales is a volume game with a personalization requirement. You need to research prospects, craft tailored outreach, analyze calls, and maintain pipeline visibility -- all while actually selling. OpenClaw is a free, open-source AI agent (165,000+ GitHub stars, MIT license) that runs locally on your machine and handles the research, writing, and analysis so you can focus on building relationships and closing deals.

OpenClaw supports Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and local models like LLaMA and Mistral. It connects through WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, a web UI, and an API. With 24/7 autonomous operation and persistent memory across conversations, it builds a growing knowledge base about your prospects, deals, and market over time.

### Prospect Research Agent

Install the **Prospect Researcher** skill from ClawHub, OpenClaw's marketplace of over 5,700 community skills. Give it a company name or domain, and it compiles a research brief covering company size, recent news, funding rounds, tech stack, key decision-makers, and potential pain points relevant to your product. OpenClaw can browse the web, call APIs, and read public data sources to build these profiles.

Set up a batch workflow where OpenClaw researches your entire prospect list overnight. You start each morning with fresh intelligence on every account instead of spending the first hour Googling.

### Personalized Outreach via Messaging

Connect OpenClaw to WhatsApp, Telegram, or your email system and use it to draft hyper-personalized outreach at scale. It takes the research it gathered on each prospect and generates messages that reference specific company challenges, recent events, or mutual connections. Every message reads like you spent ten minutes researching the person -- because your agent actually did.

OpenClaw can manage multi-touch sequences, drafting follow-ups that build on previous messages and adjust tone based on whether the prospect has engaged. Review and approve batches through Slack, and the outreach goes out on your schedule.

### Call Transcript Analysis

Feed OpenClaw your sales call recordings or transcripts (from Gong, Chorus, Otter, or raw text) and it extracts key insights: objections raised, competitor mentions, buying signals, next steps, and stakeholder sentiment. The **Call Analyzer** skill on ClawHub produces structured call summaries you can log directly to your CRM.

Set up an automated pipeline where every recorded call gets analyzed within minutes of ending. OpenClaw identifies patterns across calls -- recurring objections, common feature requests, competitive positioning gaps -- and surfaces them in a weekly digest.

### Pipeline Intelligence

Connect OpenClaw to your CRM via API and give it visibility into your pipeline. It analyzes deal velocity, stage conversion rates, and activity patterns to flag deals at risk of stalling. It can draft re-engagement strategies for stuck opportunities and recommend next actions for each deal based on historical patterns.

Schedule a daily pipeline briefing where OpenClaw reviews every active deal and delivers a prioritized action list to your Slack each morning. Persistent memory means it tracks deal progression over weeks and months, providing continuity that individual notes cannot match.

### Getting Started

Install OpenClaw from GitHub, connect your CRM and messaging channels, browse ClawHub for sales skills, and start with prospect research. Setup is free, your prospect data stays on your machine, and the agent works while you sleep.
