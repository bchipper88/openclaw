## How AI is Supercharging Sales Performance

Sales teams that adopt AI systematically aren't just working faster — they're working smarter. From lead qualification to deal closing, AI workflows help reps focus their energy on the activities that actually generate revenue.

### The Sales AI Advantage

**76% of high-performing sales teams** now use AI tools, and the results speak for themselves:

- **40% increase** in qualified pipeline from AI-powered lead scoring
- **50% reduction** in email drafting time with personalized templates
- **25% higher** close rates with AI-assisted deal intelligence
- **60% faster** proposal generation

### Beyond CRM: Building a Sales AI Stack

Most sales teams already have AI features in their CRM. But the biggest gains come from building workflows that connect AI across your entire sales process:

1. **Lead research automation** — Automatically enrich leads with company data, social profiles, and buying signals
2. **Personalized outreach at scale** — Generate customized emails that reference specific prospect pain points
3. **Deal intelligence** — Analyze conversation patterns, sentiment, and competitor mentions across deal cycles
4. **Proposal generation** — Create tailored proposals and presentations from CRM data and templates
