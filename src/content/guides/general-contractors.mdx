## How AI is Revolutionizing Construction

General contractors are discovering that AI tools can handle some of their most time-consuming tasks — from generating accurate estimates to managing subcontractor communications. The construction industry is catching up fast.

### AI Adoption in Construction

While construction has traditionally been slower to adopt new technology, that's changing rapidly. **52% of general contractors** now use at least one AI-powered tool, and those who do report significant improvements:

- **Double the estimate output** with AI-powered takeoff assistance
- **35% reduction** in administrative time per project
- **78% fewer errors** in initial cost estimates

### The Contractor's AI Advantage

The most valuable AI applications for contractors aren't about replacing skilled labor — they're about eliminating paperwork bottlenecks:

1. **Estimate generation** — Feed plans and specs into AI to generate detailed line-item estimates in hours instead of days
2. **Subcontractor communication** — Automate bid requests, change orders, and schedule updates
3. **Project documentation** — Generate daily reports, safety plans, and compliance documents
4. **Client updates** — Create professional progress reports from job site photos and notes

Our guide provides exact templates and workflows for each of these, designed specifically for how contractors actually work.
