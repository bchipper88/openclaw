## AI That Works as Hard as You Do

General contractors juggle estimates, subcontractor schedules, permit paperwork, and client updates -- often from a job site with just a phone. OpenClaw is a free, open-source AI agent with over 165,000 GitHub stars that runs on your own machine, works through WhatsApp or Telegram, and handles the paperwork side of contracting so you can focus on building.

OpenClaw supports Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and local models like LLaMA and Mistral. It runs 24/7, has persistent memory across conversations, and integrates with your existing tools through its API. Every piece of project data stays on your hardware -- no cloud subscriptions, no data leaving your control.

### Estimate Generation Skill

Install the **Construction Estimator** skill from ClawHub, OpenClaw's skills marketplace with over 5,700 community-built skills. Feed it project specs -- square footage, materials, labor categories, project type -- and it generates detailed line-item estimates with cost breakdowns. The skill can read from spreadsheets, PDFs, or even photos of handwritten notes using vision-capable models.

Over time, OpenClaw's persistent memory learns your pricing patterns, preferred suppliers, and regional cost adjustments. Your tenth estimate will be significantly faster and more accurate than your first because the agent remembers your markup percentages, typical material costs, and local labor rates.

### Subcontractor Coordination via Messaging

Connect OpenClaw to WhatsApp or Telegram and use it as your communication hub for subcontractor management. It can send bid requests to your sub list, collect responses, compare quotes, and compile the results into a decision matrix. When schedules shift, tell OpenClaw to notify affected subs with updated timelines -- it drafts personalized messages that reference each sub's specific scope of work.

Set up automated check-ins where OpenClaw messages each active sub weekly to confirm schedule, flag delays, and collect progress updates. The summaries land in your inbox without you making a single phone call.

### Permit Document Drafting

The **Permit Prep** skill on ClawHub helps draft permit applications, scope-of-work descriptions, and compliance documents. Give OpenClaw your project details and local jurisdiction, and it generates drafts that follow your municipality's formatting requirements. You review, adjust, and submit. What used to take hours of paperwork now takes minutes of review.

OpenClaw can also maintain a checklist of required inspections and permits for each project phase, sending you reminders through your messaging channel of choice when deadlines approach.

### Daily Progress Reports

At the end of each day, message OpenClaw through WhatsApp with a quick voice note or text summary of what happened on site. It transforms your raw notes into a professional daily progress report complete with work completed, materials used, weather conditions, and any issues encountered. These reports accumulate in a project log that OpenClaw can reference later when you need to generate client updates or resolve disputes.

### Getting Started

Install OpenClaw, connect WhatsApp or Telegram, browse ClawHub for construction skills, and start with estimate generation. Setup takes under an hour, runs on hardware you already own, and costs nothing. Your data stays yours.
