## Open-Source AI for People Operations

HR and recruitment teams handle high volumes of repetitive, detail-sensitive work -- writing job descriptions, screening resumes, preparing interview materials, and onboarding new hires. OpenClaw is a free, open-source AI agent (165,000+ GitHub stars, MIT license) that runs locally on your machine and automates these workflows while keeping sensitive employee and candidate data completely private.

OpenClaw supports Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and local models like LLaMA and Mistral. For HR workflows involving personal data, running a local model ensures nothing leaves your infrastructure. Access OpenClaw through a web UI, API, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, or Telegram. Its persistent memory means it learns your company's voice, values, and hiring standards over time.

### Job Description Generator Skill

Install the **JD Generator** skill from ClawHub, OpenClaw's marketplace of over 5,700 community skills. Provide the role title, level, team, and key responsibilities, and OpenClaw produces a complete job description with inclusive language, clear requirements, compensation framing, and your company's standard formatting. The skill is trained on best practices for reducing bias in job postings and flags language that may discourage qualified candidates from applying.

Because OpenClaw has persistent memory, it learns your company's tone, benefits package, and standard disclaimers. After the first few descriptions, you will spend more time reviewing than editing.

### Resume Screening Agent

Configure OpenClaw to process incoming applications against structured evaluation criteria you define -- required skills, experience thresholds, education, and role-specific qualifications. It reads resumes (PDFs, Word documents, plain text), extracts relevant information, scores each candidate against your rubric, and ranks the applicant pool. The output is a shortlist with rationale for each recommendation.

This runs locally on your hardware, so candidate personal information never touches a third-party service. OpenClaw processes a stack of resumes in minutes instead of the hours it takes to do manually, letting your recruiting team focus on the human side of evaluation.

### Interview Kit Builder

The **Interview Kit** skill on ClawHub generates role-specific interview question sets, evaluation rubrics, and scorecards. Provide the job description and desired competencies, and OpenClaw builds a structured interview guide with behavioral questions, technical assessments, and scoring criteria aligned to each competency.

It can generate different kits for different interview stages -- phone screen, technical round, culture fit, and hiring manager final. Persistent memory means it refines its output based on your feedback about which questions work well and which do not.

### Onboarding Automation

Set up OpenClaw as an onboarding assistant that guides new hires through their first weeks. It can deliver a personalized onboarding checklist via Slack or messaging, answer common questions about benefits and policies, schedule introductory meetings, and collect feedback at key milestones. The **Onboarding Agent** skill on ClawHub includes templates for engineering, sales, and general onboarding tracks.

OpenClaw can also generate personalized welcome materials, team introduction summaries, and role-specific resource guides -- all tailored to the new hire's department and level.

### Getting Started

Install OpenClaw from GitHub, choose a local model for maximum candidate data privacy, connect Slack for team use, and browse ClawHub for HR skills. The entire system is free, runs on your own infrastructure, and gives you full control over sensitive hiring data.
