## How AI is Transforming HR & Recruitment

HR teams are handling more responsibilities than ever — from talent acquisition and onboarding to employee engagement and compliance. AI tools help manage this growing scope without growing the team.

### AI in HR by the Numbers

**58% of HR departments** now use AI in at least one workflow, with recruiting leading the way:

- **45% reduction** in time-to-hire with AI-assisted screening
- **60% faster** job description creation
- **3x more candidates** evaluated per recruiter
- **35% improvement** in employee onboarding satisfaction scores

### High-Impact AI Use Cases for HR

The most valuable HR applications of AI focus on the high-volume, repetitive tasks that consume recruiter and HR generalist time:

1. **Job description optimization** — Generate compelling, inclusive job postings that attract the right candidates
2. **Resume screening workflows** — Build structured evaluation criteria and use AI to identify top candidates
3. **Interview preparation** — Generate role-specific interview questions and evaluation rubrics
4. **Onboarding automation** — Create personalized onboarding plans and welcome materials at scale
