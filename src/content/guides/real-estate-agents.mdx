## Your 24/7 Real Estate AI Assistant

OpenClaw is a free, open-source AI agent with over 165,000 GitHub stars that runs locally on your machine. For real estate agents, this means a private, always-available assistant that can generate listings, follow up with leads, integrate with your CRM, and research neighborhoods -- all without monthly subscription fees.

Because OpenClaw runs on your own hardware, client data never leaves your system. You connect it to WhatsApp, Telegram, or Slack so you can interact with it the same way you message anyone else. And with support for Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and local models like LLaMA and Mistral, you choose the AI brain that fits your budget and needs.

### Property Listing Generation Skill

Head to ClawHub, OpenClaw's skills marketplace with over 5,700 community-built skills, and install the **Property Listing Writer** skill. Feed it property details -- square footage, bedroom count, lot size, recent upgrades -- and it outputs polished MLS descriptions, social media captions, and email marketing copy in seconds. The skill reads directly from spreadsheets or CSV exports from your MLS system, so you can batch-generate descriptions for your entire inventory.

Customize the skill's tone and style once, and every listing you generate will match your personal brand. Because OpenClaw has persistent memory across conversations, it remembers your preferences, your brokerage guidelines, and even recurring phrases you like to use.

### Lead Follow-Up Automation via WhatsApp

Connect OpenClaw to your WhatsApp Business account and set up automated lead follow-up sequences. When a new inquiry comes in, OpenClaw can respond within seconds with a personalized message that references the specific property the lead asked about. It can answer common questions about price, availability, and showing times, then escalate to you when the conversation requires a human touch.

Because OpenClaw operates autonomously around the clock, leads that come in at midnight get the same fast response as those arriving at noon. You wake up to qualified conversations instead of cold leads.

### CRM Integration

OpenClaw's API capabilities let it read and write to popular real estate CRMs. It can automatically log new leads, update contact records after conversations, tag prospects by interest level, and trigger follow-up tasks. Set up a nightly routine where OpenClaw reviews your pipeline, identifies leads that have gone quiet, and drafts re-engagement messages for your approval.

### Neighborhood Research Agent

Install the **Area Research** skill from ClawHub to give OpenClaw the ability to compile neighborhood reports on demand. Ask it about school ratings, walkability scores, recent sales comps, or upcoming developments, and it pulls data from public APIs and web sources into a clean summary you can share with clients. Run this through Telegram while you are out showing properties and get answers in real time.

### Getting Started

Install OpenClaw from GitHub, pick a model (GPT or Claude for high-quality copy, a local LLaMA model for maximum privacy), connect your preferred messaging channel, and browse ClawHub for real estate skills. Within an afternoon, you will have an AI assistant that works as hard as you do -- for free.
